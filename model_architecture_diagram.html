Deepfake Video Detector Architecture

Accurate architecture diagram for the repository model: sampled video frames are processed as face-only RGB images, passed through a dual spatial-frequency image classifier, then aggregated into a single fake/real video decision.

Input10 sampled frames per video

Video Input

Frame Sampling

OpenCV reads frames; indices are selected with evenly spaced sampling.

Crop And Resize

Kaggle default uses full frame because source videos are already face-only.

224 x 224

RGB Normalize

BGR to RGB, scale to [0, 1], then ImageNet mean/std normalization.

B x 3 x 224 x 224

Video Decision

Average frame probabilities, then apply argmax for final fake/real class.

mean softmax

Preprocessing detail: in the Kaggle notebook, extracted face images are cached under /kaggle/working/face_images_224px_10f. In the backend service, the same 10-frame sampling and 224px normalization are applied directly during video prediction.

Dual-Branch Frame Classifier

Spatial Branch RGB appearance
EfficientNet-B4 model.features encoder extracts spatial artifacts and face appearance cues.
Average Pool model.avgpool compresses the spatial feature map.
Flatten Produces the EfficientNet-B4 spatial embedding. 1792D
Frequency Branch spectral artifacts
Grayscale Average RGB channels to one intensity map.
FFT Descriptor Hann window, FFT2, fftshift, log magnitude, 8 radial bins.
DCT-Like Pool Adaptive average pooling to 4 x 4 gives 16 extra low-frequency values.
Frequency MLP Linear 24->512, ReLU, Dropout 0.2, Linear 512->1024, ReLU. 1024D

Probability Aggregation

Per-Frame Logits

Classifier returns two logits for each sampled frame.

N x 2

Softmax

Convert logits into calibrated class probabilities used by the notebook and backend.

p(fake), p(real)

Mean Over Frames

Average fake and real probabilities over the 10 selected frames.

mean dim=0

Final Label

The class with the larger averaged probability becomes the video prediction.

argmax

Confidence

The maximum averaged probability is returned as confidence.

Implementation Facts For Paper Caption

Model class DeepfakeImageDetector
Backbone efficientnet_b4
Spatial dimension 1792
Frequency descriptor 8 + 16 = 24
Frequency embedding 1024
Fused dimension 2816
Output classes fake, real
Aggregation mean probabilities

Suggested caption: The detector samples 10 frames per video, resizes each frame to 224 x 224, and classifies each frame using a dual-branch architecture. EfficientNet-B4 extracts spatial features while a handcrafted frequency path encodes FFT radial-band and pooled grayscale descriptors. The 1792D spatial feature and 1024D frequency embedding are concatenated into a 2816D vector and passed through a multilayer classifier to produce fake/real logits. Video-level inference averages frame softmax probabilities before the final decision.
